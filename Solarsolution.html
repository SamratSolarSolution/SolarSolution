09 GOVERNMENT SCHEME

PM Surya Ghar:
Muft Bijli Yojana

A Government of India programme supporting eligible residential rooftop solar installations.

Under the published central financial assistance structure, eligible residential consumers in most states may receive ₹30,000 per kW for the first 2 kW and ₹18,000 for the additional 1 kW. The maximum standard CFA for an individual household is therefore ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW or above. Special-category states have a different rate structure.

  • For eligible residential electricity consumers.
  • Application is made through the official national portal.
  • Use an eligible/registered vendor and follow the portal process.
  • Final eligibility, amount and disbursement depend on current government rules and verification.
Visit official PM Surya Ghar portal ↗

Commercial systems are not covered by this residential household CFA. Always verify current guidelines and your DISCOM requirements on the official portal.
